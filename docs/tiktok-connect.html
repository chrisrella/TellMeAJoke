🎤 Tell Me A Joke

Connect your TikTok account

Authorize Tell Me A Joke to post short comedy clips to your TikTok profile using the official Content Posting API.

Connect with TikTok
Permissions requested
👤
user.info.basic — Read your TikTok display name and avatar to confirm which account is connected.
📤
video.upload — Upload video files to TikTok's servers before publishing.
🚀
video.publish — Publish uploaded videos to your TikTok profile as posts.
How it works
1
Click Connect with TikTok above to open TikTok's authorization page.
2
Log in and approve the requested permissions.
3
TikTok redirects back and an access token is saved — ready to post.
4
Videos are uploaded and published via the Content Posting API (video.upload + video.publish).
